# Clarifications Applied to AP Psychology Master Review

All clarifications from the AP Psychology CED clarification document have been applied.

## Terminology
- **All-or-none principle**: Used "all-or-none" throughout (not "all-or-nothing")
- **Reticular activating system**: Described as controlling "alertness and arousal" (not just arousal)
- **Hypnagogic**: Correct spelling used throughout (not "hypnogogic" or "hypnagogic")
- **Schedules of reinforcement**: Hyphenated correctly (fixed-ratio, variable-ratio, fixed-interval, variable-interval)

## Content
- **Spontaneous recovery**: Documented as applying to BOTH classical and operant conditioning
- **Effect size vs statistical significance**: Treated as clearly separate concepts with distinct definitions, scales, and exam warnings
- **Theory names for emotion**: Noted as outside the scope of the AP exam (James-Lange, Cannon-Bard names not required)

## FRQ
- **AAQ Part D**: Uses "Describe a potential ethical concern" language (not just "limitation")
- **EBQ scoring**: Uses "Select and use evidence" language per CED clarification

## Brain Structures
- **Brain's reward center**: Described by function only; specific structure names not required per CED
- **Prenatal development stages**: Noted as outside AP scope; focus on influencing factors
