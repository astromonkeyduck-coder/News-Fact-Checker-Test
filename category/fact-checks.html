Fact-Checks

Verified fact-checking reports on viral claims, public statements, and news stories. We investigate claims, verify sources, and provide clear verdicts to help you separate fact from fiction.

About Our Fact-Checking Process

Every fact-check at Noteworthy News follows a rigorous verification process:

  • Claim Identification: We identify the specific claim or statement being fact-checked
  • Source Verification: We trace claims back to original sources and verify their authenticity
  • Expert Consultation: We consult with subject matter experts when needed
  • Evidence Review: We examine all available evidence, including documents, videos, and official records
  • Clear Verdict: We provide a clear verdict: True, False, or Mixed/Unverified
  • Transparent Methodology: We explain our verification process and sources

Our fact-checks are comprehensive articles (600-1000+ words) that not only provide a verdict but explain the reasoning, context, and evidence behind our conclusions. We prioritize accuracy and transparency in all our fact-checking work.
