UW-Whitewater: from campus alert to all-clear

The September 2 warning was followed by an all-clear. Statements from UW-Whitewater and the city say police investigated the report and found no threat. The initial alert should be read alongside that resolution.

What changed

The reported threat was ruled out

After the initial campus alert, UW-Whitewater said police located the person, found no weapon and determined there was no threat. That all-clear supersedes the earlier warning.
