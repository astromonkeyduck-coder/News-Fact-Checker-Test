Clover Hill cheese recall: what the final FDA update says

The June recall and final August update concern the same outbreak. FDA marks it ended while advising consumers about recalled cheese still in storage.

Sources through Aug 26, 2026 Compiled by Noteworthy News from the documents below
What changedWhat we knowStill unknownOur coverageSources
What changed

FDA closes the outbreak investigation

The August 26 advisory marks the investigation complete and supplies the final reported outcomes. The earlier ongoing-investigation description is historical; the totals are not cumulative additions across updates.

FDA · Final outbreak update, August 26 ↗

What the sources establish

2 source documents
  1. 01

    Clover Hill Dairy expanded its recall on June 18 to include all of its cheese products because of possible Listeria contamination, according to the company announcement posted by FDA.

    FDA-hosted company announcement · June 18 ↗
  2. 02

    The FDA’s August 26 update marks the investigation complete and reports 15 illnesses across four states, 14 hospitalizations and one death.

    FDA · Final outbreak update, August 26 ↗
  3. 03

    FDA advises consumers not to eat or serve recalled cheese, and to discard frozen cheese if missing packaging makes it impossible to tell whether it was recalled.

    FDA · Recommendations and product identifiers ↗
The limits of this account

What remains unknown

  • FDA notes that recalled products may have reached locations beyond the listed distribution states. States reporting illnesses and places receiving products are different measures.
  • This brief cannot determine whether a particular package is affected. Compare its identifying information with the FDA’s recalled-product details.
