Miami cargo-plane crash: the story so far

A cargo flight overran a runway at Miami International Airport on September 6. The sheriff reported five deaths. Investigators have released preliminary recorder findings; the cited update does not determine a final cause.

What changed

NTSB adds preliminary recorder evidence

The September 9 NTSB update adds a preliminary sequence from the cockpit voice and flight data recorders. It is an investigative update, not a final determination of cause.

What the sources establish

3 source documents
  1. 01

    The FAA says 21 Air Flight 7598, a Boeing 767-300 cargo aircraft arriving from San Juan, overran a Miami International Airport runway around 2 p.m. local time on September 6.

    FAA · September 6 incident statement ↗
  2. 02

    WPLG Local 10 reported from the September 6 news conference that the Miami-Dade sheriff said five people had died and five others were hospitalized.

    WPLG Local 10 · Sheriff’s news conference ↗
  3. 03

    The NTSB identifies the runway as Runway 30 and lists investigation DCA26MA352 as ongoing. Its September 9 recorder-data update is preliminary and may change.

    NTSB · Investigation DCA26MA352 ↗
The limits of this account

What remains unknown

  • The cited NTSB update does not establish a final probable cause. Preliminary recorder observations should not be treated as a finding of responsibility.
  • These early updates do not establish the injured people’s later conditions. The casualty report above is attributed to the September 6 news conference.

An unanswered question is not evidence for a particular explanation.
