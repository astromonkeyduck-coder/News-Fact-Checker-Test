Sending a tip

Good tips share a shape. If you can, include:

  • What happened - specifically, with when and where.
  • How you know - firsthand, secondhand, documents, footage.
  • Anything we can verify against - photos, videos, links, records, names of others who can confirm.
  • A way to reach you for follow-up questions, if you are comfortable sharing it.
Source protection

This form and ordinary email are not anonymous submission channels. They provide your email address and message to Noteworthy News and its email provider; technical logs may also identify your connection. If confidentiality matters, describe your concern without sending sensitive material first. A request to withhold your name from publication is different from technical anonymity. Do not assume confidentiality has been agreed until you receive a reply.

Requesting a correction

Select "Correction request" in the form, or email with the story link, the specific claim you believe is wrong, and any supporting evidence. If you're right, the correction is published visibly in the story's timeline, per our standards.

Common questions

How fast will I hear back?

Response times vary. Please include the relevant story URL and supporting information so your message can be assessed. This is not an emergency service.

Can I submit a tip anonymously?

You may request that your name be withheld from publication. This form still requires an email address, and email, social platforms and hosting providers can retain identifying information. Discuss confidentiality before sending sensitive material; these channels cannot promise technical anonymity.

How do I report an error in a story?

To flag an error, include the article link, the exact statement you are questioning, and any supporting evidence. For a claim circulating elsewhere, send the original link or the most complete version available, along with when and where you encountered it. Verified errors are corrected visibly, not silently rewritten.

Do you accept guest articles or op-eds?

Occasionally, from people with verifiable expertise on a subject we cover. Pitch first - credentials, the argument, and why you're the right person to make it. Anything we publish goes through the same standards as our own work and is labeled per our editorial policy.

Can we partner with Noteworthy News?

We're open to partnerships with newsrooms, educators, and fact-checking organizations - especially work that advances media literacy. Describe your organization and the collaboration you have in mind. Note that no partnership buys influence over coverage.

Are you available for interviews or commentary?

Yes, on fact-checking, verification, and live news coverage. Select "Media inquiry" and include your outlet, topic, and deadline.
