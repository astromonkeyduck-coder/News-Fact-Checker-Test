## Identified Gaps and Recommendations

### Theme Gaps
- **NEI (National Identity)**: Missing from Units 1-4. Nationalism as a concept doesn't emerge until the French Revolution, so this is historically appropriate — but entries could note proto-national identity in earlier periods.
- **TSI (Technology/Science)**: Missing from Units 2, 3, 5, 8, 9. Military technology (Units 2, 8) and industrial technology (Units 5, 9) could be added.
- **INT (Europe & World)**: Missing from Units 3, 4. Colonial trade entries for the absolutism period and scientific contacts could be added.
- **ECON**: Missing from Unit 5. Economic causes of the French Revolution (debt, taxes) could be tagged.

### Category Gaps
- **Artistic/cultural**: Absent from Units 5-9. Romanticism, Realism, Impressionism, Modernism, and post-war culture entries needed.
- **Religion**: Absent from Units 7-9. Secularization, Kulturkampf, church-state relations in modern period need entries.
- **Nationalism**: Not tagged as category in Units 1-6. Early national identity (Tudor England, French new monarchy) could be tagged.

### Regional Gaps
- **Scandinavia**: Only 1 entry. Swedish involvement in Thirty Years' War, Scandinavian social democracy could be added.
- **Balkans**: Only 2 entries. Balkan crises leading to WWI need more entries.
- **Ottoman-European borderlands**: Not tracked as separate category. Ottoman siege of Vienna (1683), Greek independence (1821) should be added.
