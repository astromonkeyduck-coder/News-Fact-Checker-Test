How we verify

How we know what we know.

Developing means developing. Confirmed means confirmed. The two never blend.

  1. 01

    Detect

    Signals come from official feeds, agency alerts, and primary accounts we watch.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Claims trace to the original source or official data before we publish them.

  3. 03

    Context

    We state what is known, what is not, and who is doing the saying.

  4. 04

    Publish

    Every update ships with a timestamp and a source note attached.

  5. 05

    Correct

    Corrections stay visible. Nothing is silently rewritten after the fact.
