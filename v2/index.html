How We Verify

Trust is a process, not a promise.

Every story on Noteworthy News goes through the same checks, and the work stays visible: where a claim came from, when we published it, and what changed since.

Read the Full Policy
  1. 01

    Primary sources first

    Claims trace back to agencies, on-record statements, and raw data. Not screenshots of screenshots.

  2. 02

    Timestamps on everything

    Every update carries the time it was published, so you can see how a story evolved.

  3. 03

    Official statements, in context

    We quote what officials actually said, and we note what they have not confirmed.

  4. 04

    Confirmed vs. developing

    The two are always labeled and never blended. You always know which one you're reading.

  5. 05

    Corrections in the open

    When we get something wrong, the fix is visible. Nothing is silently rewritten.

  6. 06

    Source context matters

    Who is saying it is part of the story. We tell you, every time.

Why Noteworthy Exists

Built for people who want to understand the news.
