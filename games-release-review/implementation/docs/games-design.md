# The Waterline Files

The Situation Room is the flagship Noteworthy Games campaign. Its six investigations follow a fictional Merehaven estuary through a disputed map, an unexpected tracer, competing explanations and the tradeoffs of intervention. Players can inspect measurements, compute spatial relationships, test models and produce a briefing with an appropriately limited conclusion.

Three concepts were considered. Echo Bureau would pair sound travel with GIS localization and source timestamp checks; Shadow Archive would pair sunlight/occlusion with image-location verification. Waterline Files was chosen for the range of mysteries one coherent water/tracer model supports and the clear relationship between an experiment and a changed explanation.

Fact Checker: The Evidence Desk remains independently playable through Quick Check and Case Files. Geography Challenge: Atlas Expedition preserves Classic50, Hard and Typing alongside ten-targetjourneys. The collection promise is “Understand the story. Know the world.” A shared UTC Today'sDispatch and localjournal connectthepaths.
