Editorial Policy

Last Updated: October 2025

Our Mission: Noteworthy News is committed to delivering accurate, fact-checked journalism in an era of information overload. We combine traditional journalistic standards with modern fact-checking tools to provide reliable news that empowers informed decision-making.

1. Accuracy and Verification

All content published on Noteworthy News undergoes rigorous fact-checking:

  • Multiple Source Verification: We verify information using at least two independent, credible sources before publication
  • Primary Source Preference: We prioritize primary sources, official documents, and direct quotes when available
  • Expert Consultation: Complex topics are reviewed by subject matter experts
  • Correction Policy: Errors are corrected promptly with clear notation of the correction

2. Editorial Independence

Noteworthy News maintains complete editorial independence:

  • Our editorial decisions are based solely on news value, accuracy, and public interest
  • We do not accept payment or other compensation in exchange for coverage
  • Our funding sources do not influence our editorial content
  • We clearly distinguish between news reporting, analysis, opinion, and sponsored content

3. Core Editorial Principles

Truth-Seeking

We prioritize truth over speed. Accuracy is never sacrificed for the sake of being first to report.

Transparency

We clearly identify our sources, disclose any potential conflicts of interest, and explain our reporting methodology.

Fairness

We seek multiple perspectives and give subjects of our reporting an opportunity to respond before publication.

Accountability

We correct errors promptly and transparently, and we hold ourselves to the same standards we apply to others.

4. Source Attribution

We are committed to clear source attribution:

  • All sources are clearly identified unless there are compelling reasons for anonymity
  • Anonymous sources are used only when necessary and when the information cannot be obtained through other means
  • We explain why a source remains anonymous and provide as much context as possible about the source's credibility
  • We verify information from anonymous sources through additional independent verification

5. Conflicts of Interest

Our journalists and editors are required to:

  • Disclose any financial, personal, or professional relationships that could influence coverage
  • Avoid activities that could create conflicts of interest or the appearance of such conflicts
  • Refrain from accepting gifts, favors, or special treatment that could compromise journalistic integrity
  • Maintain separation between editorial and business functions

6. Fact-Checking Process

Our fact-checking process includes:

  • Pre-Publication Review: All articles are fact-checked before publication
  • Source Verification: We verify the credibility and reliability of all sources
  • Context Checking: We ensure information is presented with appropriate context
  • Update Policy: Articles are updated when new information becomes available
  • Retraction Policy: We retract articles that contain significant factual errors

7. Opinion and Analysis

We clearly distinguish between different types of content:

  • News Reporting: Objective, factual reporting based on verified information
  • Analysis: In-depth examination that may include interpretation but is grounded in facts
  • Opinion: Clearly labeled opinion pieces that express a point of view
  • Sponsored Content: Clearly identified advertising or sponsored material

8. Corrections and Updates

When we make errors or need to update information:

  • Corrections are made promptly and clearly noted in the article
  • Significant corrections are noted at the top of the article
  • We maintain a public corrections log on our website
  • We acknowledge errors transparently and take responsibility for mistakes

9. Diversity and Representation

We are committed to:

  • Seeking diverse perspectives in our reporting
  • Ensuring our coverage represents a variety of voices and experiences
  • Challenging stereotypes and assumptions in our reporting
  • Building a newsroom that reflects the diversity of our audience

10. Reader Engagement

We welcome feedback from our readers:

  • We provide clear channels for readers to report errors or raise concerns
  • We respond to substantive questions about our reporting
  • We consider reader feedback in our editorial decisions
  • We maintain a public ombudsman or reader representative role when possible

11. Ethical Guidelines

Our journalists follow these ethical principles:

  • Respect for privacy and dignity of individuals
  • Minimization of harm while reporting on sensitive topics
  • Honesty and transparency in all journalistic practices
  • Accountability to the public for our work

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about our editorial policy or wish to report an error, please contact us through our website or social media channels. We take all concerns seriously and will respond appropriately.
