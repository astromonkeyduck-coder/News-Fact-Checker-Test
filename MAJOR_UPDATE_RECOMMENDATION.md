## 🎮 Core Concept

Transform the geography game from a **one-time challenge** into a **progressive learning journey** where players:

1. **Unlock Country Profiles** by correctly identifying countries
2. **Build Mastery Levels** through repeated play
3. **Explore an Interactive Atlas** with rich country information
4. **Track Progress** across all 50 countries
5. **Earn Achievements** for milestones

---

## 📋 Feature Breakdown

### 1. **Country Mastery System**

**How it works:**
- Each country starts at **Level 0 (Undiscovered)**
- Correctly identify a country → **Level 1 (Discovered)** - Unlocks basic profile
- Identify correctly 3 times → **Level 2 (Familiar)** - Unlocks detailed facts
- Identify correctly 10 times → **Level 3 (Mastered)** - Unlocks all content + badge
- Perfect game (all 50 on first try) → **Level 4 (Expert)** - Special badge + bonus

**Visual Indicators:**
- Map shows mastery levels with color coding:
  - 🔴 Red = Undiscovered
  - 🟡 Yellow = Discovered
  - 🟢 Green = Familiar
  - 🔵 Blue = Mastered
  - ⭐ Gold = Expert

**Benefits:**
- Players have reason to replay
- Encourages learning (not just speed)
- Creates collection/achievement psychology

---

### 2. **Interactive Country Atlas**

**Unlocked Content Per Level:**

**Level 1 (Discovered):**
- Country name & flag
- Basic population
- Continent/region
- Capital city

**Level 2 (Familiar):**
- Detailed population stats
- Major cities
- Official language(s)
- Currency
- Fun fact (current system)

**Level 3 (Mastered):**
- Full country profile:
  - Geography (rivers, mountains, landmarks)
  - Culture (traditions, festivals, food)
  - History (key events, timeline)
  - Economy (GDP, industries)
  - Politics (government type, leader)
  - Current events (recent news - connects to Noteworthy News!)
- Photo gallery
- Interactive map with points of interest

**Level 4 (Expert):**
- All Level 3 content
- Expert quiz questions
- Historical timeline
- Cultural deep-dive
- Connection to breaking news stories

**Atlas Interface:**
- Beautiful card-based UI
- Search/filter by continent, mastery level
- Progress tracking (X/50 mastered)
- Shareable country cards
- "Explore" mode (browse without playing)
