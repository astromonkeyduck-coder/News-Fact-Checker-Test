Why we exist

Our purpose.

Noteworthy News is an independent publication covering breaking news, international affairs, and public safety. We are interested in how events develop, the decisions behind them, and their consequences for the people affected. Our purpose is to help readers understand these developments well enough to examine the evidence and form their own conclusions.

Public affairs can seem distant until a decision changes the price of a necessity, the security of a community, or a family’s ability to return home. We believe coverage becomes more useful when it explains those connections. Knowing that something happened is a beginning; understanding its significance requires context.

Coverage and public information

The site brings together news updates, source material, and automated summaries of agency information. These formats have different production paths. An automated update may be published without individual human review, and its source attribution should be read separately from any indication of editorial review.

Our editorial standard is to make the basis for a claim clear, retain the context needed to understand it, and correct substantive errors visibly. Readers should be able to distinguish an agency’s statement from our explanation of it and see where the available evidence leaves questions unresolved.

Learning to examine the news

Our educational guides and fact-checking and geography games extend this purpose. They invite readers to examine the wording of a claim, consider its sources, and understand the places involved in a story.

These are skills with uses beyond a single article. They help people assess public arguments, recognize the limits of an explanation, and participate more thoughtfully in the decisions affecting their communities.

Independence and contact

Noteworthy News is independently operated and supported by advertising, including Google AdSense. Coverage is free to read. Advertising does not give an advertiser authority over editorial decisions.

Richard Adkins is a co-founder of Noteworthy News. For editorial questions, news tips, or correction requests, contact richard@noteworthynews.co.
