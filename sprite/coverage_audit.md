## 10. Identified Gaps and Recommendations

### Priority 1: Add More Entries
- Unit 6 (Industrialization): needs 4-6 more entries (textile mills, steam power, factory acts, public health)
- Tier 2/3 rebalancing: add ~30 enrichment entries across all units

### Priority 2: Regional Gaps
- Balkans: add entries for Balkan crises, Congress of Berlin, Yugoslav Wars
- Scandinavia: add Swedish Thirty Years' War involvement, Scandinavian welfare state model
- Ottoman borderlands: add Siege of Vienna (1683), Greek independence (1821)

### Priority 3: Category Gaps
- Artistic/cultural: add Baroque, Romanticism artists, Realism, Impressionism, Modernism, existentialism
- Religion in modern units: add Kulturkampf, secularization, Vatican I/II

### Priority 4: Dashboard Enhancements
- Comparison split-screen view (currently shows comparison buddy link, not side-by-side)
- SVG causation arrows between cards
- CCOT horizontal timeline bands visualization
