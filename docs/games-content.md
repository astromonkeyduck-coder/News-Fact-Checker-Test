The authored setting is the fictional Merehaven estuary. All documents, charts, frame descriptions, institutions and claim contexts are original educational simulations. They are not news records, public emergency guidance, or quotations attributed to real institutions. No external evidence URL is invented.

Edition: `waterline-files-1.0.0`. Human editorial review is pending. There is no named human reviewer or asserted review date.

Every case has three to five evidence records per snapshot, plausible reasons, a specific verification question, decisive evidence limited to two records, a verdict explanation, remaining uncertainty and the next useful measurement. Verdict, evidence/reason and verification receive 60/30/10 percent of the stage allocation. The case total remains 100 even when later evidence changes the answer. Confidence and time never increase the score.

The Quick Check bank contains 24 independent fictional claims with six each of Supported, Misleading, False and Insufficient evidence. They use the same evidence/verification contract as flat 100-point stages. The deterministic selector avoids recent IDs where the bank permits and the daily plan locks both content editions and selected IDs through UTC midnight.

All numbers in the short cases are authored fictional source material, not asserted output from the interactive simulation. The long missions instead bind values generated by the numerical engine. Optional chart, citation, table and timestamp metadata support inspectable evidence views without replacing the full source text.

## Maintenance

See `games/CONTENT-CONTRACT.md` for field names, provenance rules, engine parameter names and observation binding. Content lives independently of presentation in `games/content.js`. Give every record a stable ID and explicit version; do not change a live attempt's content snapshot. Increment a substantive content version when its evidence or rubric changes. Do not retroactively judge an earlier stage against evidence that arrived later.

Run `node --test tests/games/content.test.js tests/games/mission-rubric.test.js tests/games/season-physics.test.js tests/games/session.test.js` when changing this bank. The tests validate the full catalog, source/diagram arithmetic, evidence bounds, fixed evolving-case totals, hidden measurement policy, tolerated versus discriminating fits, actual one-factor comparisons, unchanged probe locations, tracer source/time separation and the required final input envelope. Session tests cover corrupt-save preservation, exact-once awards, weighted score sums, daily locking, same-mode independent recall at least 24 hours apart and cross-tab notebook merging. These do not establish real-world forecasting accuracy or substitute for rendered gameplay tests.

The first-slice gate was completed before season expansion: the opening Case File was answered, refreshed between stages and saved at 100; the integrated mission retained actual GIS analysis, two full-duration runs and their comparison through refresh before a bounded 100-point briefing was filed. This is observed implementation evidence, not a claim that a human editorial board approved the content.
